# Games

Public product facts. Packs can also be read on [zadtop.com/games](https://www.zadtop.com/games/).

## Free Fire — `freefire`

Redeem onto a player ID. Packs: 100 · 210 · 530 · 1,080 · 2,200 diamonds. A check reports unused, used, or invalid, and the pack when you ask for `with_value`.

Players from almost every country are supported. Where Garena sells no vouchers — the United States, Argentina, Chile and Russia — lookup answers `422 UNSUPPORTED_REGION` before a code is sent.

## PUBG Mobile — `pubg`

UC packs from 60 to 8,100. A check can include the value, the time of use, and the player when the shop knows them.

## Yalla Ludo — `ludo` and `ludogold`

Diamonds and Gold, from $2 to $500. Instant redeem requires `tier` (a quantity, or a Diamonds USD price such as `2`). Gold is sent as its quantity.

Diamonds codes can be checked. Gold has no check. Never send a Gold code to `game=ludo`: it can be spent.

## Jawaker — `jawaker`

Token packs from 4,250 to 825,000, redeemed by player number. There is no standalone code check. A 12-character pin cannot be checked, and a code is not sent a second time if the answer is lost.

## iTunes — `itunes`

Check only. A live Apple Gift Card comes back `valid` with its dollar balance in `value_label`. A spent card reads `used`. Apple does not tell an invalid card from an already-redeemed one, so both read `invalid`. No player ID. The first read after a quiet spell can take up to a minute; later reads are quicker.

## Razer Gold check — `razer`

A PIN comes back `valid`, `used`, or `invalid`. Checking does not spend it. No player ID and no pack. **5 requests per PIN.**

## Razer Gold recharge — `razer_recharge`

The same PIN, sent to **your** wallet, so a live PIN is credited. Include `email` and `password`. The password signs the account in and is not stored. A failed sign-in, a banned wallet, a wallet that is not activated yet, or a wallet already at its limit sends no PIN and is not charged.

A credited PIN is `recharged` (amount in `value_label` when Razer reports it). A spent PIN is `used`. A wrong PIN is `invalid`. **5 requests per PIN that got a verdict.**
